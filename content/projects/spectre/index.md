Team design project analyzing and optimizing a supersonic phase-change ejector refrigeration cycle.

- Created computational models of various refrigeration cycles
- Analyzed a variety of working fluids for each cycle design
- Gave recommendations of components that are most beneficial to improve cycle efficiency
- Proposed an optimized design via solving an optimization problem numerically
