abstract: "Introduces WOLF, an algorithm combining receding-horizon model predictive control with an open-loop differential games solver via sequential convexification to solve nonconvex, game-theoretic motion planning problems with disturbances over extended time horizons. The robustness tube is treated as a dynamic state, co-optimized with the trajectory, with sufficient conditions ensuring nominal trajectories remain feasible despite all admissible disturbances. Validated on two adversarial orbital scenarios with combined translational-attitude dynamics: a jamming game under detection probability constraints and a sun-blocking scenario."
