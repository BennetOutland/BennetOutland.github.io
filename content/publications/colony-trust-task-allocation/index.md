abstract: "This paper solves a multi-agent, multi-task allocation problem wherein the agents have uncertainty in their capabilities and their capabilities degrade upon use. We consider a large, complex multi-agent task broken down into atomic sub-tasks and arranged via a signal temporal logic (STL) directed task graph. The algorithm, Capability-based Optimization through Local Observation, Outcome tracking, and Networked sYstems (COLONY), is introduced to: learn the dynamic capabilities of the agents via trust-based consensus, allocate tasks to agents or groups of agents, and do so in a decentralized manner. This approach is demonstrated on Mars rover survey, satellite repair, and lunar habitat construction problems."
