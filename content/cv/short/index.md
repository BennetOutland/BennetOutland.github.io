research_interests: >
  My research interests are in differential game theory, POMDPs, amortized optimization, optimization under uncertainty, and astrodynamics. Due to the difficulty and computational limitations associated with operating in the space domain, guarantees for safety and optimality are needed for the advancement of multi-agent space systems. My work centers on developing computationally efficient numerical methods for multi-agent systems to provide aforementioned guarantees. This has lead into decomposing difficult autonomy challenges via task allocation, solving differential games, and amortized optimization to speed up computation.
