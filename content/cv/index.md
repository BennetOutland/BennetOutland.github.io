research_interests: >
  My research interests are in Differential Game Theory, Multi-agent Systems, Astrodynamics, Stochastic Analysis, and Inverse Problems. Due to the difficulty and expense associated with operating the space domain, guarantees for safety and optimality are needed for the advancement of multi-agent space systems. My work centers on developing computationally efficient numerical methods for multi-agent systems, through the lens of Differential Game Theory, to provide aforementioned guarantees. This spans from multi-agent open-loop control, closed-loop control, inverse games, multi-agent task allocation, and advancing other capabilities like robustness and more.
